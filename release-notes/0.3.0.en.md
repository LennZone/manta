## Manta now speaks 7 languages

- **Deutsch, English, 简体中文, Español, Français, Italiano, Русский:** pick one in Settings › General › Language or let Manta follow your system language – it switches instantly. The manual (⌘?) is available in all 7 languages too
- **Dragging now copies by default.** When you drop, Manta asks "Copy" or "Move" – Copy is preselected, hold ⌘ to move. Settings offer "Always copy" or "Always move"
- **Ask on name conflicts:** like Finder – Replace, Keep Both, Skip, or Merge for folders, optionally for all further conflicts
- **See where you are:** instead of "Local" the path bar shows "Docker · …", "SFTP · …", "Google Drive" and so on – with an arrow back to your Mac
- **Progress at a glance:** a small indicator in the status bar shows running transfers; files in cloud folders show when they're still uploading or the upload failed
- **Complete menu bar** with every command and shortcut – also for shortcut cheat-sheet apps
- **"Manta as default file manager"** now also in the Manta menu and the command palette
- A distinct icon for "Hidden Files"; the filter field folds into a magnifier in narrow panes (⌘F)

## Manta 0.4.7 – eject, mouse side buttons, crash fixed

- **Eject drives like in Finder:** disk images, USB sticks and external drives are listed in the sidebar under **Ejectable**, with their own icon and an eject button. The context menu offers Open, Open in New Tab, Show in Enclosing Folder (for a DMG, the file) and Eject. New drives appear by themselves. Thanks to Lennart Novak!
- **Back and forward with the mouse's thumb buttons:** buttons 4 and 5 go back and forward in the pane under the pointer, in Help too. Settings → General → Files → **Mouse side buttons** swaps them or turns them off
- **Crash fixed:** renaming with only one pane open made Manta quit

## Manta 0.2

A big update – Manta now feels even more like a real Mac file manager.

- **As many panes as you need:** no longer just two – split the window with ⌘D and close panes with ⇧⌘D
- **Four views with thumbnails:** Icons, List, Columns and Gallery (⌘1 – ⌘4)
- **Drag folders into your favourites** – and reorder them there by dragging
- **Drag & drop with Finder and other apps,** both ways – hold ⌥ to copy and ⌘ to move, just like in Finder
- **Pause and resume transfers:** they even survive a lost network connection and pick up where they left off
- **Delete to the Trash** with ⌘⌫, delete immediately with ⌥⌘⌫
- **Finder's shortcuts:** ⌘T for a new tab, ⌥⌘L for transfers, ⌘Y for Quick Look
- **Folders refresh by themselves** as soon as something changes in them
- **Passwords in the Keychain:** server credentials are now stored safely in the macOS Keychain
- **Safer SSH:** Manta checks the server's key and warns you if it changes
- **Cloud in the sidebar:** iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive and Dropbox through their apps – with badges that show what's already on your Mac
- **The manual is built in:** ⌘? opens it, with search and links to every topic
- **Manta stays free.** If you like it, you can buy me a coffee via **Help** → **Support Manta ☕ …** – entirely optional.

**Permissions one last time:** Manta now has a permanent signature. That's why macOS asks for access once more after this update, for example to Documents or Desktop. Just confirm – from now on your choices stay in place across future updates.

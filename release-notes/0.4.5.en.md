## Manta 0.4.5 – free space, iCloud Drive like Finder

- **Free space in the status bar:** "12 items · 18 GB available" – on your Mac the disk's space (like Finder), in iCloud Drive and Google Drive what's left of your account, on SFTP and SMB servers their disk, on WebDAV (Nextcloud, for example) your quota. The tooltip shows the total size
- **iCloud Drive like Finder:** the top folder also shows your apps' folders by their name ("Obsidian", "Pages", "Shortcuts") instead of `iCloud~md~obsidian/Documents`; the path bar starts at "iCloud Drive" instead of `com~apple~CloudDocs`
- **App folders are protected:** like in Finder, they can't be renamed, moved or deleted; what's inside them can

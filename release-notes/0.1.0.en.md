## Welcome to Manta

The first release of Manta – the dual-pane file manager for Mac.

- **Two panes with tabs:** copy, move and compare files, including drag & drop
- **At home everywhere:** your Mac, SFTP/FTP servers, S3 storage, Docker containers and Kubernetes pods
- **A terminal under every pane** that follows your folder – inside containers and on servers too
- **Git at a glance**, folder sizes, batch rename, Markdown preview, archives as folders
- **Quick Look on the space bar**, just like Finder
- **Plugins** you can write yourself, in English and German

From now on Manta keeps itself up to date.

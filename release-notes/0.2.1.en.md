## Trash in the sidebar

- **Trash under "Locations":** shows whether it's empty or full and opens with one click
- **Drag onto it:** dropping files on the Trash moves them there – from other apps too. On servers Manta asks first, because deleting there is permanent
- **Empty Trash…** from the right-click menu or with ⇧⌘⌫, with a confirmation
- Without Full Disk Access, Manta shows how to allow it – or opens the Trash in Finder
